Everyday wellbeing

Small habits support your health

Build a balanced plate

When available, include a variety of foods such as vegetables or fruit, grains, and a source of protein.

Drink water regularly

Keep water nearby and drink throughout the day, especially when you feel thirsty or are active.

Make time to move

Choose movement that feels comfortable for you. Short walks and stretch breaks can count.

Give yourself time to rest

Regular sleep and breaks are part of looking after your overall wellbeing.

These are general wellbeing tips, not personal medical advice. Choose what works for you.
